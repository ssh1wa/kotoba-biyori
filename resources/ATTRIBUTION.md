# Open Learning Content Attribution

The grammar JSON files in `hanabira-data/grammar_json` are sourced from the
[Hanabira Japanese content repository](https://github.com/tristcoil/hanabira.org-japanese-content).

Hanabira states on its [About page](https://hanabira.org/about) that its
in-house vocabulary, grammar, and example-sentence files may be used for
personal or commercial purposes for free with credit to hanabira.org, and
describes the files as Creative Commons material. The upstream pages do not
identify a specific Creative Commons license version.

The imported Hanabira files retain those upstream terms and are not relicensed
under KotobaBiyori's PolyForm Noncommercial license. See
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the complete
third-party notice.

KotobaBiyori's lesson sequence, scenes, goals, and roleplay prompts are
independently written. Commercial textbook readings, dialogues, illustrations,
audio, exercises, and complete vocabulary lists are not included.
